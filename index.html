Welcome to Open Mathematics

Mathematics is often taught as a set of formulas to memorize, but in reality, it is a creative universe of patterns, logic, and discovery. Open Mathematics is a free digital magazine created to bridge the gap between basic arithmetic and awe-inspiring mathematical ideas.

Whether you are a student, hobbyist, or just curious about how numbers shape our world, our articles are written without heavy jargon so anyone can jump right in.

  4. Zero: The History of Nothing – How a simple placeholder revolutionized human thought.

Question: Is 0.999... (repeating) equal to 1?

Answer: Yes! In mathematics, 0.999... is exactly equal to 1. One quick way to see this: 1/3 = 0.333... If you multiply both sides by 3, you get 3/3 = 0.999..., which means 1 = 0.999...
