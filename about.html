Our Mission

Open Mathematics was created to make mathematics open, inviting, and easy to explore. We publish short articles, logical paradoxes, and historical breakdowns that require no advanced degree to understand.

Submission Guidelines

We welcome contributions from students, teachers, and math enthusiasts! Before submitting, keep these criteria in mind:

  • Articles should be between 300 and 800 words.
  • Math concepts must be explained in plain English.
  • Topics can cover pure math, puzzles, cryptography, or mathematical history.
